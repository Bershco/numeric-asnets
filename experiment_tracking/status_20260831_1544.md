## MPrime corrective-rescore defect

Preflight `20768884` completed but was a false pass.  The legacy wave evaluator
passed a multiprocessing Queue to a worker entry point changed by the lifecycle
refactor to call `send()` on a Pipe.  Every worker crashed with
`AttributeError: 'Queue' object has no attribute 'send'`, while the wrapper
still wrote a `.done` marker.  Full-array task `20768902_0` reproduced the
failure; the other 27 tasks remain memory-pending.

Local repair:

- one compatibility sender supports both Pipe `send()` and Queue `put()`;
- a regression test covers both contracts;
- the rescore wrapper refuses worker-crash logs and requires a nonempty summary
  before writing `.done`.

No MPrime anchor score from these jobs is scientifically valid yet.
