## Horizon, SAFE and determinism

- SAFE-1 repaired two of four targeted Drone dead-end failures and remains a
  useful safety invariant, not a complete search-quality fix.
- SAFE-CONTEXT is a completed negative experiment: splitting nodes by action
  history reduced useful sharing and hurt VH-on coverage. It is not deployed as
  the default behavior.
- The fresh determinism audits found identical external decisions for repeats
  on one CPU family. Different CPU families changed policy and child-statistic
  checksums, but changed no selected action in the audited instances. A fresh
  same-node Horizon-aware/unaware replay was identical with zero cutoffs. Thus
  random timing is not supported as the cause of the old three-instance Horizon
  difference; that old difference remains attributable only to uncontrolled
  historical build/process differences, not to the Horizon flag.
- The live Counters Horizon jobs still record zero cutoffs. A likely reason is
  that each individual MCTS search is much shallower than the remaining
  external action budget, even late in a long trajectory. SAFE2 `(state,h)` is
  therefore demoted to held-low-priority: it cannot affect behavior until
  nonzero binding cutoffs and cross-horizon state reuse are demonstrated.
