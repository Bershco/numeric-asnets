## Outage recovery decisions

- Delivery job 20755722 failed because the compute node could not allocate a multiprocessing semaphore (`ENOSPC`), not because of model logic. Its valid Stage-2 snapshot 87 is preserved. Correct VH-off continuation 20821028 runs only the remaining 13 epochs; controller 20768207 now depends on it. An accidentally submitted VH-on continuation 20821013 was cancelled before start and produced no evidence.
- Zenotravel policy job 20812836 was an operational requeue/hold after user-environment retrieval failed. It was released and is running normally.
- TPP job 20684890 reached the 72-hour scheduler limit after snapshot 79. The tested timeout fallback treated that snapshot directory as its declared terminal endpoint. Controller 20821306 recognized all 16 held-out lineages and submitted the 17 missing every-five/endpoint policy jobs.
- Corrected MPrime anchor validation resumed its final 48 points. Array tasks 4 and 15 run. Index 0 again received SIGILL on an `ise-cpu-intl` node and was resubmitted as 20821691 on `ise-cpu128-11`, where it is running. MPrime Stage 2 remains held until all 588 points are reviewed and per-VH coefficients are frozen.
- Rover OOM job 20559583 was deduplicated by instance and action sequence. Job 20821803 completed in three seconds: five unique printed plans, all five VAL-valid. Its fixed-budget result is therefore 5/20 with an OOM outcome, not ten successes.
- The causal Horizon determinism audit was deployed and submitted. Preflight 20821796 gates three aware and three unaware repeats 20821797--20821802 on the same checkpoint, instance, node and deterministic settings.
