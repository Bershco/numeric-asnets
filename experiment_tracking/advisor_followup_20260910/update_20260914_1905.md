## TPP first-update crossover

The latest smoke failure was not scientific evidence and not a node-capacity
failure. Profiling tried to mutate the frozen initialization record. Commit
`a6c773f2` limits those fields to mutable worker outputs and was deployed to
the isolated checkout. Replacement smoke `21267359` gates the two missing
one-epoch crossover cells `21267360[0-1]`, then endpoints `21267361[0-1]`.

The nonzero KL before weights changed does not mean coefficient selection or
policy evaluation used the wrong mode. During Stage-2 optimization, the
current-policy side of the KL loss uses dropout (`training=True`) while the
frozen anchor is deterministic (`training=False`). Equal weights can therefore
emit different probabilities. Coefficient 3 was still selected correctly
under the declared pipeline; the finding instead shows that the training KL
term partly penalizes dropout disagreement as well as weight drift. The
crossover deliberately preserves that behavior to isolate checkpoint versus
replay causality.
