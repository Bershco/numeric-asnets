## Completed TPP causal result

The selected catastrophic TPP/off seed fell from Stage-1 20/20 to 10/20 after
the historical first Stage-2 epoch. A frozen-replay crossover showed that the
susceptible starting checkpoint was necessary in the tested pair; replay
identity changed the severity. The corrected one-epoch treatment restored the
catastrophic endpoint to 20/20 and retained the stable control at 20/20.

This was not gradient clipping. The treatment changed two things:

1. it measured anchor KL with deterministic current and anchor policies rather
   than including sampled dropout disagreement; and
2. it rejected excessive Adam steps, restored weights and optimizer state,
   reduced the learning rate, and recomputed the same data batch.

The catastrophic arm used 11 backtracks across 60 accepted updates; the stable
control used four. This is strong selected-pair prevention evidence, not a
population estimate or proof that learning-rate scaling alone caused the
repair. Exact dropout-RNG closure and a broader one-epoch non-regression screen
remain documented but unsubmitted.

## Why fixed/PW search uses 20 or 70 simulations

The normal code heuristic is

`simulations = clip(10 + 3 * retained_children, 10, 200)`.

With 20 retained children this yields 70 simulations. The narrow 5-child
configuration would yield 25 automatically, but the thesis deliberately uses
the historical 5-child/20-simulation low-budget comparator for expensive Block
Grouping and Counters cells. PW70 matches the normal 20/70 compute budget;
PW20 matches the narrow 5/20 override. These results are labelled separately
and never pooled.
