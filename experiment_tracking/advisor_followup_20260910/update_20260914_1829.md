## TPP catastrophic first-update audit

Phase A is complete: the bad seed reproduced 20/20 to 10/20 after one Stage-2
epoch, while the stable control remained 20/20. The bad update had 43% higher
mean per-step KL, 75% higher maximum KL and 21.5% more cumulative parameter
movement. Both arms lost the same worker slot and used the same replay ingestion
order, so worker identity/count and completion order do not explain the split.

The frozen-replay crossover runs only the two missing off-diagonal cells: bad
checkpoint with stable replay and stable checkpoint with bad replay. It repeats
no target generation and no 100-epoch training. The first smoke lacked the
native operator; the repaired second smoke verified all 120 batches and passed
five loader tests, then encountered native exit `-4` on
`ise-cpu-intl-10`. Final replacement `21265386` excludes only the three nodes
with documented native failures and gates `21265387[0-1]` and
`21265388[0-1]`.

The nonzero KL before any weight update does not mean coefficient selection was
performed in evaluation mode incorrectly. Inside the Stage-2 training loss, the
trainable/current policy forward pass uses training mode with dropout, whereas
the frozen anchor forward pass is deterministic. Identical starting weights can
therefore emit different probabilities and a nonzero KL. This is separate from
policy evaluation and from the prior validation-led choice of coefficient 3.
It does mean that the anchor is not a pure measure of parameter drift. Phase B
retains this behavior to avoid confounding checkpoint/replay attribution; a
later deterministic-current or rollback treatment can test whether changing it
prevents the collapse.
