### Why the Rover jobs have not finished at the historical median

The 21 jobs currently span approximately 19--29h. Their logs already contain
roughly 9--14 actual six-hour instance timeouts each, processed in rolling
waves of three workers. Fifteen timeout instances alone imply about 30h of
wall time even with three concurrent workers. Several jobs have started only
16--19 of twenty instances. A historical median near 30h therefore predicts a
cluster of completions around 30--36h; it did not imply every job would finish
before 30h.

## Determinism conclusion

Same-node repeats were identical. Switching from `cs-cpu-07` to
`ise-cpu-intl-07` changed network-policy tensors at 78/105 decisions and child
statistics at 45/105 but changed no selected action. The evidence localizes
variation to CPU/node-dependent floating-point network execution, not process
timing and not search depth. Hardware kernels can alter predictions without
necessarily altering behavior. A further causal test should use a known
selected-action-divergent instance and compare pinned node types.

The best first follow-up candidate is Drone VH-on seed `1239739722`: fresh
same-commit jobs `20684991` (unaware) and `20684992` (aware) scored 9/20 and
6/20 even though the aware arm recorded zero horizon cutoffs. The next static
step is to diff their per-instance success membership and choose an instance on
which the action sequence actually diverged. It is a stronger candidate than
seed `1073581256`, whose aware arm recorded a real cutoff, because the horizon
mechanism itself should have been inactive for seed `1239739722`.
