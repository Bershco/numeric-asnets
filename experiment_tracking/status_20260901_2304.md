## Determinism conclusion

Same-node-family repeats were identical at every external decision. Moving
between CPU families changed network and child-statistic checksums, but changed
no selected action in either audited instance. A fresh same-commit, same-node
aware/unaware replay followed the identical 165-action failing trajectory with
zero cutoffs. The historical Drone Horizon difference was not reproduced;
neither process timing nor the nonbinding Horizon flag is supported as its
cause. It remains historical environment/build variation of unknown exact
source, not evidence of a Horizon effect.
