### Determinism audit

Both six-run audits are complete. Repeats within a node family are exact.
Changing from cs-cpu-07 to ise-cpu-intl-07 reproducibly changes network-policy
and child-statistics checksums, but selected actions, physical states, action
histories and outcomes remained identical on both tested instances. The newest
target failed 0/1 in exactly 165 actions in all six repeats. Therefore hardware
numeric variation is real but has not been shown to change behavior; random
process timing and CPU family alone do not explain the historical Horizon
coverage divergence. Exact evidence is in
`mcts_determinism_audit/followup_results.csv`.
